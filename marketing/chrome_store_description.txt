find my accounts is a privacy-first account discovery tool with local processing and data security as a primary feature.

Out of the box, find my accounts discovers forgotten online accounts by analysing your email exports (MBOX files) entirely on your device. It identifies accounts, providing:

- Direct links to account deletion pages
- Exportable results

Additionally, because everything runs locally in your browser, your sensitive email data never leaves your computer, ensuring complete privacy while you take back control of your online presence.

In beta — features may change; feedback welcome.
