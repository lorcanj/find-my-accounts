find my accounts

Find every account linked to your email address.

Scan your email archive to see every service and subscription you've signed up for — then get direct links to delete each one. Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Download extension View source

Free & open source  ·  Chrome  ·  No permissions required

How it works

  1. 1
    Export your email

    Download your inbox as an .mbox file from Gmail (Google Takeout), Thunderbird, Apple Mail, or Proton Mail.

  2. 2
    Upload the file

    Open the extension and pick your .mbox file. The scan runs entirely inside your browser — no server involved.

  3. 3
    Discover & delete

    See every account found, with difficulty ratings and direct links to deletion pages — powered by the JustDeleteMe dataset.

Everything runs in your browser.
Nothing is ever uploaded.

No permissions  ·  No tracking  ·  Open source

What you get

  • Complete picture

    Finds accounts from registration emails, billing receipts, password resets, and more.

  • Deletion links built in

    Matched against the JustDeleteMe directory — easy, medium, or hard difficulty shown for each service.

  • Export your results

    Download a full CSV or JSON of every account found to use however you like.

  • Works on large archives

    Streaming parser handles multi-GB .mbox files without freezing your browser.
